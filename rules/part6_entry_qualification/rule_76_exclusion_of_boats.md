# Rule 76: Exclusion of Boats or Competitors

**Part 6 — Entry and Qualification**

**Source:** Racing Rules of Sailing 2021-2024, World Sailing  
https://www.sailing.org/inside-world-sailing/rules-regulations/racingrules/

---

## 76.1

The organizing or race committee may reject entries provided they do so "before the start of the first race after receipt of the entry" and provide proper reasoning. Written justification must be supplied upon request.

## 76.2

Rejection is prohibited based on:
1. Advertising compliance with the World Sailing Advertising Code
2. World and continental championships when entries meet stated quotas and relevant Class Association or World Sailing approval exists

## 76.3

Boats or competitors may request redress if they believe rejection or exclusion violates rule 76.2 or is improper.
