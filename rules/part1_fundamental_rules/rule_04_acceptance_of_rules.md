# Rule 4: Acceptance of the Rules

**Part 1 — Fundamental Rules**

**Source:** Racing Rules of Sailing 2021-2024, World Sailing  
https://www.sailing.org/inside-world-sailing/rules-regulations/racingrules/

---

## 4.1

By participating in an event conducted under these rules, each competitor and boat owner agrees to accept them. Support persons providing assistance, or parents/guardians permitting children to enter, similarly agree to acceptance.

## 4.2

Competitors and boat owners agree on behalf of their support persons that such persons are bound by the rules.

## 4.3

Acceptance includes agreement to:
- Be governed by the rules
- Accept penalties as final determinations
- Not resort to courts outside the rules framework
- Ensure competitors and owners that support persons understand their obligations

## 4.4

"The person in charge of each boat shall ensure that all competitors in the crew and the boat's owner are aware of their responsibilities under this rule."

## 4.5

National authorities may modify this rule through prescription.
