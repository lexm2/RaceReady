# Rule 53: SKIN FRICTION

**Part 4 — Other Requirements When Racing**  
**Section B — Equipment and Clothing**

**Source:** Racing Rules of Sailing 2025-2028, World Sailing  
https://www.sailing.org/inside-world-sailing/rules-regulations/racingrules/

---

A boat shall not eject or release a substance, such as a polymer, or
have specially textured surfaces that could improve the character of
the flow of water inside the boundary layer.
