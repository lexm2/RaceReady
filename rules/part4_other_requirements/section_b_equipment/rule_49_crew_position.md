# Rule 49: Crew Position; Lifelines

**Part 4 — Other Requirements When Racing**  
**Section B — Equipment-Related Requirements**

**Source:** Racing Rules of Sailing 2021-2024, World Sailing  
https://www.sailing.org/inside-world-sailing/rules-regulations/racingrules/

---

## 49.1

"Competitors shall use no device designed to position their bodies outboard, other than hiking straps and stiffeners worn under the thighs."

## 49.2

When required by class rules or other regulations, crew cannot position torsos outside lifelines except briefly for necessary tasks. On dual-line setups, competitors may sit facing outward with waist inside the lower line while upper body extends beyond the upper line. "Unless a class rule or any other rule specifies a maximum deflection, lifelines shall be taut." Lifelines must comply with World Sailing Offshore Special Regulations specifications unless class rules specify otherwise.
