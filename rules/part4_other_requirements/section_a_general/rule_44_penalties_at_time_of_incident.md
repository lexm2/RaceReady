# Rule 44: Penalties at the Time of an Incident

**Part 4 — Other Requirements When Racing**  
**Section A — General Requirements**

**Source:** Racing Rules of Sailing 2021-2024, World Sailing  
https://www.sailing.org/inside-world-sailing/rules-regulations/racingrules/

---

## 44.1 Taking a Penalty

A boat may take a Two-Turns Penalty for potentially breaking Part 2 rules, or a One-Turn Penalty for potentially breaking rule 31. The notice of race or sailing instructions may specify alternative penalties. However:

1. When breaking both Part 2 rules and rule 31 in the same incident, the rule 31 penalty need not be taken
2. If injury, serious damage, or significant racing advantage resulted despite penalty, retirement is required

## 44.2 One-Turn and Two-Turns Penalties

"After getting well clear of other boats as soon after the incident as possible, a boat takes a One-Turn or Two-Turns Penalty by promptly making the required number of turns in the same direction, each turn including one tack and one gybe." At the finishing line, the hull must be completely on the course side before finishing.

## 44.3 Scoring Penalty

1. If specified, a boat takes this penalty by displaying a yellow flag at the first reasonable opportunity
2. The flag remains displayed until finishing; the boat must inform race committee at the finish line and identify the other boat involved (or do so within the protest time limit if impracticable)
3. The boat's score is her original score made worse by stated points (or 20% of Did Not Finish if unstated, rounded to nearest tenth), but not worse than Did Not Finish
