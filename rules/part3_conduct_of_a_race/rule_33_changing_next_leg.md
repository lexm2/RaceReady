# Rule 33: Changing the Next Leg of the Course

**Part 3 — Conduct of a Race**

**Source:** Racing Rules of Sailing 2021-2024, World Sailing  
https://www.sailing.org/inside-world-sailing/rules-regulations/racingrules/

---

While racing, the race committee may change a leg beginning at a rounding mark or gate by repositioning the next mark or finishing line before boats begin that leg. The new position need not be established beforehand.

**Direction changes** are signaled with "flag C with repetitive sounds" plus either a new compass bearing or:
- Green triangle: starboard change
- Red rectangle: port change

**Length changes** are signaled with "flag C with repetitive sounds" plus:
- Minus sign: decreased
- Plus sign: increased

**Subsequent legs** may be modified without additional signals to maintain course shape.
