# Rule 35: Race Time Limit and Scores

**Part 3 — Conduct of a Race**

**Source:** Racing Rules of Sailing 2021-2024, World Sailing  
https://www.sailing.org/inside-world-sailing/rules-regulations/racingrules/

---

If at least one boat completes the course within the designated time limit, all finishing boats are scored by their finishing positions unless the race is abandoned.

If no boat finishes within the time limit, the race committee must abandon the race.
