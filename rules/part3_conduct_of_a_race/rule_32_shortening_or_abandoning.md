# Rule 32: Shortening or Abandoning After the Start

**Part 3 — Conduct of a Race**

**Source:** Racing Rules of Sailing 2021-2024, World Sailing  
https://www.sailing.org/inside-world-sailing/rules-regulations/racingrules/

---

## 32.1

After the starting signal, the race committee may shorten the course or abandon the race due to:
- Foul weather
- Insufficient wind preventing timely completion
- Missing or misplaced marks
- Other safety or fairness concerns

The committee may also shorten the course to accommodate scheduled races or abandon due to starting procedure errors. However, after any boat completes the course within the time limit, abandonment requires considering consequences for all participants.

## 32.2

To shorten the course, the race committee displays "flag S with two sounds" before the first boat crosses the finishing line. The new finishing line is either:
- At a rounding mark between the mark and a staff displaying flag S
- A line the course requires crossing
- At a gate between the gate marks

## 32.3

To abandon a race in progress, the race committee displays "flag N, N over H, or N over A, with three sounds."
