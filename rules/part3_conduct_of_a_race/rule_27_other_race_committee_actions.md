# Rule 27: Other Race Committee Actions Before the Starting Signal

**Part 3 — Conduct of a Race**

**Source:** Racing Rules of Sailing 2021-2024, World Sailing  
https://www.sailing.org/inside-world-sailing/rules-regulations/racingrules/

---

## 27.1

By the warning signal, the race committee shall designate the course if not previously stated, may replace course signals, and may signal that personal flotation devices are required using "flag Y with one sound."

## 27.2

The race committee may relocate a starting mark no later than the preparatory signal.

## 27.3

Before the starting signal, the race committee may postpone (displaying "flag AP, AP over H, or AP over A, with two sounds") or abandon the race (displaying "flag N, N over H, or N over A, with three sounds") for any reason.
