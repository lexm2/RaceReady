# Rule 89: Organizing Authority; Notice of Race; Appointment of Race Officials

**Part 7 — Event Organization**

**Source:** Racing Rules of Sailing 2021-2024, World Sailing  
https://www.sailing.org/inside-world-sailing/rules-regulations/racingrules/

---

## 89.1 Organizing Authority

Races shall be organized by:

1. World Sailing
2. A member national authority of World Sailing
3. An affiliated club
4. An affiliated organization (other than club), with national authority approval if prescribed
5. An unaffiliated class association, with national authority approval or in conjunction with an affiliated club
6. Two or more of the above organizations
7. An unaffiliated body with affiliated club ownership/control (subject to national authority prescription)
8. An unaffiliated body with affiliated club (if World Sailing and national authority approve and body lacks ownership/control)

An organization is affiliated if connected to the venue's national authority; otherwise unaffiliated. For multi-jurisdiction racing, affiliation exists through any national authority port of call.

## 89.2 Notice of Race; Appointment of Race Officials

**(a)** "The organizing authority shall publish a written notice of race that conforms to rule J1."

**(b)** "The notice of race may be changed provided adequate notice is given."

**(c)** "The organizing authority shall appoint a race committee and, when appropriate, appoint a protest committee, a technical committee and umpires." World Sailing may appoint race committee, international jury, and umpires under its Regulations.
