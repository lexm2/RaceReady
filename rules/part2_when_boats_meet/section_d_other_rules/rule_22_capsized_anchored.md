# Rule 22: Capsized, Anchored or Aground; Rescuing

**Part 2 — When Boats Meet**  
**Section D — Other Rules**

**Source:** Racing Rules of Sailing 2021-2024, World Sailing  
https://www.sailing.org/inside-world-sailing/rules-regulations/racingrules/

---

A boat should "avoid a boat that is capsized or has not regained control after capsizing, is anchored or aground, or is trying to help a person or vessel in danger."

A "boat is capsized when her masthead is in the water."
