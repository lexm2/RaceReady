# Rule 19: Room to Pass an Obstruction

**Part 2 — When Boats Meet**  
**Section C — At Marks and Obstructions**

**Source:** Racing Rules of Sailing 2021-2024, World Sailing  
https://www.sailing.org/inside-world-sailing/rules-regulations/racingrules/

---

## 19.1 When Rule 19 Applies

Rule 19 applies between two boats at an obstruction except when Rule 18 applies and the obstruction is either a mark or another overlapped boat. At continuing obstructions, Rule 19 always applies and Rule 18 does not.

## 19.2 Giving Room at an Obstruction

A right-of-way boat may choose which side to pass an obstruction on. If changing course to do so, "she shall give the other boat room to keep clear."

When overlapped, the outside boat must give the inside boat room between herself and the obstruction, unless "unable to do so from the time the overlap began."

If a boat clear astern becomes overlapped between the other boat and a continuing obstruction with insufficient room, she gets no entitlement to room under 19.2(b) and "shall keep clear" while overlapped.
