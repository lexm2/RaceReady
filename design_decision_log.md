- **Decision:** Designed the prototype in light mode. **Rationale:** Light mode is faster to prototype and easier for reviewers to read during the design review session. The final product will have a light and dark mode option with the default being the dark theme.

- **Decision:** Used the University of Michigan color palette. **Rationale:** The primary user the platform was designed around is a Michigan collegiate sailor. Using Michigan colors makes the platform feel relevant and familiar to that audience.

- **Decision:** Chose Montserrat for headings and IBM Plex Sans for body text. **Rationale:** Montserrat's geometric style fits the competitive sports vibe while IBM Plex Sans prioritizes screen readability.

- **Decision:** Added three CTA buttons on the home screen in addition to the top navigation. **Rationale:** The three core features (studying, whiteboard, and games) are the primary reasons a user opens the app. Surfacing them as buttons on the home screen reduces the number of clicks to get started and makes the app's purpose immediately clear.

- **Decision:** Faked the game and quiz interactions rather than building functional prototypes. **Rationale:** Building interactive logic in Figma for a timed game and a branching quiz is not feasible within the scope of a lo-fi prototype. The static screens communicate the intended experience well enough for reviewers to give feedback on layout and structure of the features.
